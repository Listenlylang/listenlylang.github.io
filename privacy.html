We do not collect your payment card details, location, contacts, photos or microphone audio. We do not show third-party ads and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. Word translations shown in the player are made on your device.

Service providers

  • Supabase — database, sign-in and file storage; stores your account and learning data.
  • Google Sign-In and Sign in with Apple — sign-in only.
  • RevenueCat — manages subscriptions and purchases; receives your user id and purchase receipts.
  • Google Play and the Apple App Store — process all payments.

Storage and security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored with Supabase. Sign-in tokens on your device are kept in the system's secure storage.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app under Settings → Account → Delete account, or by email — see Delete your account. Deletion removes your account, progress, settings and our subscription record. Purchase records kept by Google or Apple follow their own policies. Technical logs that do not contain your user id are kept for up to 90 days.

Subscriptions

Deleting the app or your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in Google Play or the App Store.

Children

Listenly is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, or object to its processing. Write to abdomamdou4@gmail.com.

Changes

When this policy changes we update this page and its effective date.
