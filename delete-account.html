Delete your Listenly account

Applies to Listenly and every Listenly language app — they share one account: Listenly, Listenly English (US), Listenly English (UK), Listenly Arabic, Listenly Spanish, Listenly French, Listenly German, Listenly Japanese, Listenly Korean, Listenly Italian, Listenly Chinese, Listenly Portuguese, Listenly Russian, Listenly Turkish and Listenly Dutch. Developer: Listenly.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open the app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account → Delete account.
  3. Read the notice, tick “I understand…” and tap Delete account.

Your account is deleted right away and you are signed out on that device.

Can't open the app?

Email abdomamdou4@gmail.com from the email address of your account, with the subject “Delete my account”.

We delete the account and confirm by email within 30 days.

Delete some data only

To keep your account but delete part of your data (for example your learning progress, saved lessons or saved words), email abdomamdou4@gmail.com from your account's email address and say what to delete. We complete it within 30 days.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription bought in Google Play or the App Store. Cancel it first so you are not charged again.
  • Google Play: Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • App Store: Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address, sign-in identities such as Google or Apple, user id).
  • Learning progress, lesson history, streaks, saved lessons and words, and app settings.
  • The subscription status we store for your account, and your customer record at our payment provider RevenueCat.
  • On the device: the app's saved settings, sign-in data and cached files.

What may be kept

  • Purchase records held by Google or Apple (their own policies apply).
  • Technical logs without your user id (for example, a lesson's audio failed to load), kept for up to 90 days.
  • Anything we must keep by law (for example, tax records of purchases), only for as long as required.
